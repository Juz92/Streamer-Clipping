# ADR 0002: Publish without a creator-permission hard gate

## Status

Accepted — 2026-07-14

## Context

Official-policy research found that the current Daily Peanut edits are real visual/editorial work but do not guarantee copyright permission or YouTube Partner Program reused-content eligibility. Written permission plus commentary-led edits was recommended as the lowest-risk model, but Justin chose to accept the documented risk rather than make either condition mandatory.

## Decision

The system may publish BurntPeanut clips without written creator permission once its other editorial and technical promotion gates pass. This is a **Risk-Accepted Publish**, not a rights clearance. The current format—selected complete arcs, stream-specific split-screen crops, dynamic captions, frame-zero hooks, thumbnails and layout changes—remains the intended original contribution; original narration/commentary is not mandatory.

Any removal request from TheBurntPeanut/Loaded or any copyright claim triggers the **Claim Kill Switch**: unpublish the affected clip and do not file a dispute, appeal or counter-notice unless clear written rights evidence covers the claimed material. The channel must not represent itself as official or affiliated without permission.

## Consequences

- This is explicit risk acceptance, not a finding that the format is legally safe or YPP-eligible.
- The channel may be rejected or removed from YPP under reused-content rules, even without copyright claims.
- Content ID claims, takedowns and strikes remain possible; third-party music, guests, game assets and watched-on-stream material can create rights issues TheBurntPeanut cannot necessarily resolve.
- Autonomous posting must preserve the Claim Kill Switch and a source/claim audit trail.
- A future written partnership may supersede this decision.
